# Sphere - a concealed black hole drifting through TDA

Appears now and then. It is the LAST layer (ink_layer -> nin_stage -> sphere -> viewer), so the NIN acts and echoes
never distort it, while its own gravity bends everything behind it. You notice it by what it does to the space round
it far more than by itself - no rim, no rings.

- **When:** 35-75 s after it left (Intervalmin / Intervalmax) it waits for the next strong hit of track 1 (or 6 s),
  grows out of a point over Grow (3 s), stays 14-22 s (Durationmin / Durationmax) while it drifts along a meandering
  path through the frame (Travel), swelling and shrinking (Sizechange), then collapses back.
  Summon Now / Dismiss Now on the Sphere page; Enable off = it stays away.
- **The hole:** round - a soft, vague darkness (Size 0.05 of the frame width, Conceal 0.75).
- **Gravity (most of all close to it, reaching only about Reach = 3 horizon radii - it stays local):** the space sags towards it (Lens) and twists
  round it into a vortex (Swirl, strongest close in); close to it the scene is torn into
  radial streaks falling into it (Streaks - spaghettification) and seethes with turbulence; slow gravitational waves run
  out on hits; light near it is dimmed. The Ambient Ink becomes its plasma meanwhile (Plasma): born on its edge, pulled
  back into arcs and loops (ink_track2: Solarpull), swirling and flowing round it.
- **Beams** (Beam, Beamrate ~9 s): its two beams along the axis only flash for a split second (0.2-0.35 s) now and then
  (on a hit, or 3 s after it is due), spinning round fast while they last. No eruptions.
